## [0.5.0] - 2026-09-23

### Added
- **Headless Editorial Figure Composition**: A headless figure engine composes publication figures entirely in physical millimetres — panels dimensioned from their content apertures, axis-aligned placement and containment on the sheet, deterministic z-ordering, and invertible framing and sheet-placement transforms — with under-specified geometry refused with typed errors rather than guessed: rotated medical panels are not yet placed, and every transform traces to a ratified decision instead of an invented convention.
- **Fail-Closed Publication Render-Request Assembly**: Every publication render begins from a validated, frozen request assembled from a composed figure sheet and exactly one prepared view per panel. Source availability is resolved under a declared policy only — live requests require every panel online, offline requests require an explicit offline-preview allowance and mixed sheets are refused — and offline panels are admitted only after value-equal cached-preview references, order-independent fingerprint-set equality and a verified render-state hash. Malformed input of any kind is refused with a typed, diagnostic-bearing error, never a crash, and the assembled request is accepted by the ratified publication contract oracle.
- **Patient-Anchored Annotation Projection**: Annotations anchored in patient space are projected onto the figure sheet through the ratified physical chain — patient LPS geometry to the displayed imaging plane, viewport, panel content and sheet millimetres — consuming the authored coordinate transforms without ever re-deriving them. Out-of-plane visibility follows a ratified policy: exact-plane visibility with a linear fade band or a hard cutoff, and any non-online source hides the annotation fail-closed while still reporting the physical out-of-plane distance. Hidden annotations emit nothing; no screen pixel is ever persisted.
- **Publication Renderer Port and High-Resolution Medical Capture**: Medical panel imagery enters the figure engine only through a caller-supplied renderer port, keeping the engine free of any graphics context or rendering library. A live orchestration derives each panel's temporary render target from its physical aperture at the requested DPI and captures through the real medical rendering harness — verified at 600 DPI producing a native 1890×1890 raster for an 80 mm panel — without ever resizing the live interactive canvas; rasters with wrong dimensions, wrong byte length or wrong renderer identity are refused outright rather than upscaled.
- **Flattened Edition Export (PNG/TIFF)**: A pure sheet compositor flattens the figure into an opaque raster by deterministic alpha-over compositing, never resampling — a layer whose pixel dimensions do not match its physical destination at the plan DPI is refused — and a pluggable encoder port exposes the flattened result to deterministic PNG and TIFF writers built on the standard compression library. Encoded bytes are byte-identical across repeated runs, and both writers are verified by decoder round-trips.
- **Hybrid Vector PDF Export**: Publication figures export as hybrid PDF documents in which the medical panels are embedded as high-resolution raster images at their exact physical rectangles while typography, panel letters, backgrounds, borders, lines, ellipses and polygons are emitted as native vector primitives using an embedded open-licensed font — the page is never flattened. Document metadata is deterministic with a content-hash document identifier, repeated encodes are byte-identical, and the full paint order — panel background below the medical imagery, annotations and borders above it — is explicit data rather than an implementation accident.
- **Deterministic Editorial-to-Vector Mapping**: Every piece of editorial figure content — panel backgrounds and borders, labels and captions, text and panel-letter boxes, line annotations (sheet, panel-content and patient-anchored) and region-of-interest ellipses and polygons — maps deterministically to native PDF vectors through the ratified projection transforms, inventing no geometry. Editorial styles without a ratified semantic — arrowheads, scalebars, measurement ticks, dashed or dotted borders, bold text, unsupported fonts, multi-line text boxes and patient-anchored ROI or text shapes — are refused with typed errors and explicitly deferred rather than approximated.
- **Architectural Governance & Evidenced Closure**: The figure engine, its physical-units and framing contracts, the export encoder pipeline and the editorial vector mapping are governed by ratified architectural decision records, and the milestone closure was verified by an independent review and quality-assurance pass over the accumulated delivery — including the package's UI-free, rendering-library-free boundary, the no-resampling and determinism invariants enforced across four independent layers, and byte-identity evidence for PNG, TIFF and PDF — with every executable verification gate green. Evidence was gathered under a software WebGL 2 renderer; hardware-GPU rendering, a production desktop bundle and pixel-tolerance comparison on real clinical datasets remain outside the verified scope of this release, and the deliberately deferred capabilities listed above are carried as tracked follow-ups rather than silent gaps.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-09-23

### Added
- **Deterministic Mutual-Information Rigid Registration**: Automatic rigid registration between a fixed and a moving imaging volume, computed entirely within the scientific worker under a fully ratified deterministic protocol — a multi-resolution mutual-information metric with geometry-based centre initialisation, a fixed iteration budget with a convergence-based stopping rule, a pinned numerical imaging stack and strictly single-threaded execution — so repeated runs within the same locked runtime environment are bitwise identical. The recovered alignment is returned as a proper rigid transform mapping source points onto target points in patient millimetre coordinates, and the operation never falls back, extrapolates or fabricates a result: invalid input evidence, a failed or non-finite optimisation and any non-rigid outcome are refused with typed, diagnostic-bearing errors. The capability is exercised end-to-end against the real scientific worker on curated synthetic volume pairs with independently verified ground truth, while the resulting evidence remains validated scientific evidence only — its admission into cross-study view propagation is deliberately reserved for a separate future decision rather than being silently assumed.
- **Fail-Closed Registration Evidence Validation**: Every transform produced by the scientific worker is admitted to the application layer only after fail-closed validation on both sides of the bridge. The matrix must consist of sixteen finite values with a homogeneous last row, and the declared transform type must agree with the actual matrix — a rigid claim requires a proper orthonormal rotation with unit determinant, an identity claim must be the identity itself, and a legitimate affine claim requires only a well-formed matrix; units must be millimetres; scientific provenance — computation method, worker version and timestamp — must be complete; a residual margin is accepted only when finite and non-negative; and the source and target frames of reference must be present and distinct. Degenerate configurations are refused outright rather than approximated — degenerate landmark sets such as too few correspondences or coincident and collinear points, registration against the source's own frame of reference, and a best fit that would require mirroring instead of rotating — and every mismatch between declared and actual transform properties fails closed with a typed error naming the refusal, never a silently corrected result.
- **Verified Real-Source Pixel and Volume Transport**: The scientific worker now decodes real DICOM pixel data and the headless medical engine hydrates the validated result into its existing volume ingestion and residency path, under a ratified transport contract that keeps the scientific worker the only DICOM pixel decoder: the application layer accepts declared pixel bytes and geometry evidence verbatim and never parses DICOM, rescales voxels or derives geometry a second time. The worker fingerprints every selected source before publication — an exact content digest of the source instances, an instance-identity digest and a verified frame of reference — and refuses non-conformant or internally inconsistent sources; payloads travel as binary temporary files behind opaque, single-owner handles, with mandatory content hashing, exact byte-length verification, expiry anchored to descriptor publication and checked on every read, bounded byte budgets, automatic invalidation of outstanding handles on worker restart and no file access outside the private temporary root. The engine re-validates the descriptor, hash, length and expiry before any ingestion plan is built, treats the accepted geometry evidence as the sole geometry authority and verifies quantitative PET payloads against registered asset semantics without rescaling data in transit. Fail-closed limits are part of the contract rather than gaps: compressed or otherwise unsupported pixel representations, multi-sample pixel data, metadata inconsistent with the declared geometry and over-budget volumes are refused with typed errors instead of being approximated, and the whole worker-to-engine chain is verified end-to-end on curated synthetic CT and PET fixtures containing no patient data.
- **Architectural Governance & Evidenced Closure**: The registration, evidence-validation and pixel/volume-transport capabilities are governed by ratified architectural decision records, and the milestone closure was verified by an independent review and quality-assurance pass over the accumulated delivery — including the fail-closed refusal paths and the synthetic, patient-data-free fixture evidence — with every executable verification gate green. Production hardware-GPU and real-Windows worker-runtime evidence remain outside the verified scope of this release and are reported honestly as not yet applicable rather than claimed.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-09-22

### Added
- **Headless Imaging Workspace**: A pure, UI-agnostic imaging workspace that decides what to show, in which logical slot and with which state and relations — without owning pixels, GPU resources or any user interface. Studies and imaging assets are registered into a fully serializable model, view slots are organised as up to four groups of four clinical roles (maximum-intensity projection, PET, generic and fusion) for at most sixteen logical slots, binding and unbinding follow explicit, fail-closed status transitions between empty, bound, prepared and unavailable states where every illegal transition is refused with a typed error, and study and asset intake is integrity-checked at the boundary: non-finite numbers, non-JSON-safe values (dates, maps, class instances, cyclic structures) and explicit undefined values are refused with typed errors naming the exact offending path before any workspace state is touched.
- **Immutable Prepared-View Assembly**: Every published view is assembled from a clinical view state plus provenance evidence and published as a validated, deep-frozen, JSON-lossless object that consumers cannot mutate. Provenance is correlated one-to-one by position — each source asset, series and fingerprint entry describing the same source within a single study — and missing or mismatched correlation is refused before anything is published. Binding a prepared view to a slot is a separate, explicit, fail-closed operation, and assembly alone pins no memory or graphics resources: residency is declared separately, never implied by holding a view.
- **Shared-State View Groups**: Several views can reference one shared spatial-and-camera state pair owned privately by the engine instead of an imperative notify chain. Updates happen only through atomic replacement — the replacement payload is validated and frozen, a fresh frozen projection is regenerated for every member view, and the group, view and slot identities stay stable so consumers re-read by identity — while any refused update leaves the shared pair and every bound view object-identical.
- **Physically Distinct View Linking**: Synchronisation between views is admitted only through physically distinct link modes, and every mismatch fails closed. Within one study, co-referenced linking requires the same worker-verified frame of reference together with a one-to-one correlation of geometry snapshots to assets, series and fingerprints — an equal geometric digest is deliberately not required, because native CT and PET series sharing one frame of reference legitimately differ. Across studies, relative and transformed linking require a declared spatial differential or a validated spatial transform, an explicit tolerance in millimetres and a declared out-of-domain policy. Applying a link is delivered for co-referenced pairs only — both views then share one frozen spatial-and-camera state — while cross-study application and propagation remain future work rather than being silently approximated.
- **State Locks and Local View Overrides**: Any named view state (spatial, camera, presentation, projection, composition or binding) can be locked, and every canonical mutation of the shared spatial-and-camera state is refused with a typed error naming the protected state, its owner and the operation — never partially applied. A local view override, by contrast, resolves a per-view divergence into a fresh, frozen, serializable view state with deep clinical validation of every substituted value and coherence checks for composition variants; it never mutates the source view and never writes to any registry, and a lock protecting canonical state deliberately does not block a purely local divergence.
- **Persistent Viewport Surfaces and Placement Geometry**: A persistent, WebGL-agnostic surface registry gives every viewport a stable surface and viewport identity independent of any graphics context, with an explicit lifecycle across available, mounted, hidden and disposed states in which a binding travels with its surface, disposal is terminal and a disposed surface carries no binding; the sixteen-surface capacity is a lifetime logical identity budget, never a count of WebGL contexts. A pure, deterministic placement manager derives viewer-grid and composer-panel host rectangles in caller-supplied units and preserves surface identity across re-layout, refusing non-finite or overflowing output geometry — including coordinates that overflow although every input is finite — instead of publishing corrupted placement.
- **Declarative Resource Demand Projection**: Prepared views declare what imaging material they need, and the engine projects that demand into the medical engine's residency manager: visible, bound or prepared slots project retention requests under stable, caller-owned lease identities derived only from logical slot and asset identity and encoded collision-free so two distinct demands can never merge into one; a shared asset is retained once while serving several views; eviction preserves each view's semantic identity and on-demand reload restores residency; and hidden or inactive slots release their claims at the next reconciliation. The view engine owns no residency policy — acquisition, budgets, eviction ordering and byte accounting remain with the residency manager, whose physical ingestion plan stays behind a caller-supplied attachment seam — and malformed or untyped projection input is refused with typed errors before any residency can change.
- **Architectural Governance & Evidenced Closure**: The workspace, prepared-view, linking, lock, surface and residency capabilities are governed by ratified architectural decision records, and the milestone closure was verified by an independent review and quality-assurance pass over the accumulated delivery — including the UI-agnostic, DOM-free package boundary and every architectural invariant — with every executable verification gate green.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-09-21

### Added
- **Headless Medical Rendering Engine**: A real, UI-agnostic Cornerstone3D volume-rendering path inside the headless medical engine, exercised end-to-end through a controlled WebGL 2 harness — an environment without WebGL support fails closed with an explicit unavailability error instead of degrading, every started renderer tears down cleanly with typed, retryable lifecycle errors, and the deterministic software rasterizer is reported honestly as software rather than presented as hardware acceleration.
- **Explicit Series-to-Volume Loading**: Imaging volumes are constructed only from accepted scientific-worker evidence — availability, series classification, patient-space geometry and the full pixel-payload contract (scalar type, bit layout, dimensions and finite values) are validated before any rendering structure is created — so unsupported classifications, missing or mismatched evidence, geometric disagreement and malformed payloads are refused with typed errors and never reach the renderer.
- **Resource Residency Management**: A declarative residency manager governs which imaging volumes remain materialized, using lease-based shared resources so a fused view pins its CT and PET sources independently, byte-level budget accounting that reports an unmeasurable capacity axis honestly instead of guessing, deterministic priority-ordered eviction confined to unreferenced resources, and on-demand reload that preserves each asset's identity and provenance; a fail-closed terminal disposal completes the lifecycle, with no global cache purge anywhere.
- **Medical View State Application**: The persisted medical view state — CT, PET and CT/PET fusion presentations — is applied to live three-dimensional viewports through one shared rendering path conforming to the PET/CT fusion radiometry specification, with ratified DICOM palettes resolved by stable identifier before any property is written, the viewport's pixel size measured from the mounted element, residency verified against the real renderer cache, and fail-closed refusal of frame-of-reference mismatches, unsupported spatial transforms, non-neutral cameras and unsupported slice positioning rather than silently approximating them.
- **Fusion Presentation Contracts**: A per-layer composition contract with single, fusion and multi-layer states expresses CT/PET fusion declaratively — the PET overlay's overall opacity has a single authoritative source, transfer modes and blend bounds are validated, and incomplete or ambiguous compositions are rejected instead of being inferred.
- **Quantitative PET Radiometry Binding**: Quantitative fusion is admitted only through a ratified binding — PET modality, computed body-weight quantitation against a positive SUV factor, and pixel data declared in the rescaled Bq/mL transport domain — with the single, tested SUV↔activity conversion and typed refusals for every unmet condition, so raw-count or CT-domain PET data is never silently fused or colourized.
- **DICOM Palette Catalog**: The four nuclear-medicine palettes from the DICOM standard's well-known palette color lookup tables (Hot Iron, PET, Hot Metal Blue, PET 20 Step) ship as a ratified declarative catalog with canonical stable identifiers, idempotent typed registration, and whole-table regression digests that detect a change of even a single byte.
- **Ordinary Medical Raster Capture**: Any applied view can be captured to a native-resolution raster carrying complete semantic provenance — view identity, per-layer asset, volume, role, modality, palette, intensity domain and window, composition mode, orientation and renderer facts — with a runtime verification that every PET layer actually exposes its scalars in the quantitative Bq/mL transport domain before pixels are accepted, and with independently applied views provably isolated from one another.
- **Temporary High-Resolution Render Target**: A publication-density render target is dimensioned directly from physical millimetres and target DPI, applies the exact same view state through the ordinary rendering and capture path at native pixel dimensions, never resizes or otherwise mutates the live interactive canvas, releases its temporary resources deterministically, and refuses with a typed error rather than falling back to upscaling a preview.
- **Architectural Governance & Evidenced Closure**: Each new rendering, radiometry, palette, capture and publication-target capability is governed by a formal architectural decision record, and the milestone closure was verified by an independent review and quality-assurance pass over the accumulated delivery, with every executable verification gate green.

## [0.1.2] - 2026-09-20

### Added
- **Headless Scientific Worker Bridge**: A UI-agnostic bridge within the headless medical engine that supervises the full lifecycle of the local Python scientific worker over the versioned stdio JSON-RPC protocol — from deterministic startup handshake through clean shutdown — with concurrent request correlation, per-request timeouts, and automatic recovery through bounded restart backoff. The handshake verifies protocol-version compatibility and the worker's advertised scientific operations before any request is served; requests fail closed while the worker is not ready, and an incompatible or failed handshake aborts deterministically rather than falling back.
- **Verbatim Scientific Result Mapping**: The worker's scientifically computed results — study and series classification, physical patient-space geometry, PET body-weight quantitation, and pairwise geometry-compatibility evidence — are translated into NuClear clinical contracts with scientific provenance preserved and every computed value copied verbatim. The bridge performs no scientific computation of its own: no formula, DICOM interpretation, or quantitative result is ever re-derived in the application layer.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-20

### Added
- **Figure & Publication Contracts**: Serializable figure composition, millimetre-spaced panels, discriminated physical annotations (LPS patient anchors and sheet coordinates), and multi-format publication targets (high-resolution TIFF/PNG and vector-preserving hybrid PDF).
- **View & Viewport Contracts**: Coordinate transform pipelines from Patient LPS space to view plane and viewport pixels, decoupled view slots, stable viewport surface identities, and inter/intra-study synchronization linking with granular locks.
- **Clinical Data Contracts**: Pure domain contracts for multimodal studies, imaging assets, LPS physical patient geometry with outer half-voxel boundary definitions, nominal branded identifiers, and source locators for local folders and archives.
- **Quantitative PET Contracts**: Strict acquisition metadata and decay correction specifications, reserving numerical calculations and quantitation provenance for the scientific processing engine.
- **Automated Verification Harness**: Headless contract verification suite and curated oncology PET/CT fixtures asserting geometric and relational invariants.
- **Unified Documentation Portal**: Integrated TypeDoc API reference and Python documentation tooling accessible via a centralized portal.
- **Repository Architecture Foundation**: Monorepo structure with seven decoupled domain packages, architectural governance directives, and headless-first verification standards.
- **Python Scientific Worker Baseline**: A provisioned local scientific worker environment, declared package documentation, a versioned manifest that distinguishes established evidence from planned work, and versioned protocol request, response and error examples.

### Changed
- Enforced strict nominal typing across domain contracts, eliminating ambiguous primitive string unions.
- Reconciled patient coordinate bounds to use an exact 3D axis-aligned bounding box enclosing all eight oriented voxel corners.

### Security
- Hardened study contracts against Protected Health Information (PHI) leakage by removing direct patient identifiers from persisted project references.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-09-20

### Added
- **Scientific Worker Protocol**: A versioned newline-delimited stdio JSON-RPC protocol for the local Python scientific worker, with a deterministic handshake, a structured error schema that always carries a diagnostic and never a result on failure, mandatory worker provenance on success, and a stateless supervisor that answers every record, isolates diagnostics from the protocol channel and never terminates on malformed input.
- **DICOM Study & Series Inspection**: Metadata-only discovery that groups instances into studies and series and explicitly classifies primary CT, attenuation-corrected PET, uncorrected PET, localizer, secondary-capture and unsupported series; every non-primary series is reported with a reason and nothing is silently discarded, while unreadable sources fail closed.
- **Regular-Grid Geometry Extraction**: Deterministic patient-space geometry reporting grid dimensions, spacing, orientation, origin, normalized slice ordering and the physical bounding envelope of the oriented voxel corners, together with pairwise cross-series compatibility evidence derived from frame-of-reference and slice coplanarity.
- **Fail-Closed Geometry Hardening**: Geometry is rejected when coordinates, dimensions or spacing are non-finite or non-positive, when study, frame-of-reference or modality identity is inconsistent within a series, or when instance identities are duplicated; outgoing protocol records use strict JSON so non-finite values cannot escape the transport.
- **PET SUV Body-Weight Quantitation**: Production of the body-weight standardised uptake value factor with mandatory scientific provenance, reading radiopharmaceutical dose and half-life from the radiopharmaceutical information sequence defined by the DICOM standard, applying decay correction referenced only to the start of acquisition and using date-aware acquisition timestamps; missing, unusable or unsupported acquisition metadata yields an explicit invalid or unavailable result instead of an estimate.
- **Acquisition-Time and Numeric Conformance**: DICOM date-time offsets are validated against the permitted range for the standard, and PET numeric inputs must be finite and positive at both the scientific boundary and the persisted domain type guards.
- **Developer Tooling**: Semantic code navigation across the repository and a monorepo version-synchronisation script that keeps all workspaces, the Python worker and the versioned protocol examples on a single release version.
